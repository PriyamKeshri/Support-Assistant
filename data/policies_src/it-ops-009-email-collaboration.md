# Email and Collaboration Standard
Policy ID: IT-OPS-009 | Version 1.8 | Owner: Workplace Technology | Effective: 1 January 2026

## 1. Mailboxes
Every employee receives a 100 GB mailbox and a 50 GB online archive. Email attachments are limited to 25 MB; share larger files with a OneDrive or SharePoint link instead.

## 2. Shared Mailboxes and Distribution Lists
Request a shared mailbox (for example support@ or finance@) or a distribution list through an Access Request. Each one needs two named owners, who approve its membership. Distribution lists with more than 500 members, and all-company lists, accept email only from approved senders such as Internal Communications.

## 3. Microsoft Teams
Any employee can create a team for a project. Teams with no activity for 180 days are archived after the owner has been notified. Guests from outside Kestrel can be invited only to teams marked External, and guest access is reviewed every 90 days.

## 4. Calendars and Meeting Rooms
Book meeting rooms through Outlook. A booking that is not checked in on the room panel within 10 minutes of its start time is released. Recurring room bookings expire after 6 months and must be renewed.

## 5. Email Security
Emails from outside the company are tagged [EXTERNAL] in the subject line. Messages with executable attachments, such as .exe, .js and .iso files, are blocked. Encrypt any email that contains Confidential data by using the Encrypt button in Outlook. Restricted data must not be sent by email at all (see IT-SEC-005).

## 6. Signatures and Out-of-Office Messages
Use the standard email signature from the brand portal. Set an out-of-office message for any absence of 2 or more working days, and name a backup contact in it.
