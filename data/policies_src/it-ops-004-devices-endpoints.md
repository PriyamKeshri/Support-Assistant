# Device and Endpoint Policy
Policy ID: IT-OPS-004 | Version 3.1 | Owner: IT Operations | Effective: 1 February 2026

## 1. Standard Hardware
- Engineering and data roles receive a MacBook Pro 14-inch with 32 GB of memory.
- All other roles receive a MacBook Air 13-inch with 16 GB of memory, or a Lenovo ThinkPad T14 for roles that depend on Windows.
- Laptops are refreshed every 4 years, and engineering laptops every 3 years. Earlier replacement needs a hardware fault confirmed by the Service Desk.
- One external monitor, keyboard and mouse are provided on request. Desks and chairs are covered by the home-office allowance in the HR handbook.

## 2. Encryption and Device Management
Every laptop must be enrolled in Microsoft Intune and must have full-disk encryption turned on: FileVault on macOS and BitLocker on Windows. Recovery keys are stored in Intune, and the Service Desk can retrieve one after verifying your identity. Devices that are not enrolled cannot sign in to company applications.

## 3. Installing Software
Install software from the Company Portal self-service catalogue. If an application you need is missing, raise a Software request; new software is reviewed for security and licensing within 5 business days. Local administrator rights are not standard. You can request temporary admin rights through a Privileged Access Request; they are granted for 4 hours at a time and every use is logged.

## 4. Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal phones and tablets may be used for email, Teams and the Outlook calendar through the Intune app protection policy, without enrolling the whole device. On a personal device, the company can wipe only work data, never personal data. Personal laptops and desktops must not be used to access source code, the data warehouse or any Restricted data.

## 5. Lost or Stolen Devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop, security key or phone with work data on it within 2 hours, by calling the Service Desk on ext. 4357 or by raising a P1 ticket from another device. IT will lock the device and, if it is not recovered within 24 hours, wipe it remotely. If the device was stolen, file a police report and add the report number to the ticket within 3 days.

## 6. Patching and Compliance
Operating system and browser updates must be installed within 14 days of release, and critical security updates within 72 hours. Devices that are more than 21 days out of compliance are blocked from single sign-on until they are updated.

## 7. Returning Equipment
When you leave Kestrel Analytics, return your laptop, security keys and accessories within 5 business days of your last working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid shipping box. Equipment that is not returned is deducted from the final settlement at its depreciated value.
