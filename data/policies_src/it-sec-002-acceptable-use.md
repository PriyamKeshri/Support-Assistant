# Acceptable Use Policy
Policy ID: IT-SEC-002 | Version 2.4 | Owner: Information Security | Effective: 15 January 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy covers how employees, contractors and interns may use Kestrel Analytics devices, networks, accounts and SaaS tools.

## 2. Personal Use
Limited personal use, such as personal email, news or online banking during breaks, is acceptable as long as it does not affect your work, use significant bandwidth or break any other policy. The following are not permitted on company devices or the company network:
- cryptocurrency mining;
- peer-to-peer file sharing and torrent clients;
- gambling websites;
- storing personal photo, music or film libraries on a company laptop.

## 3. Prohibited Software
- Unlicensed or pirated software.
- Personal or consumer VPN services. KestrelConnect is the only VPN allowed on company devices.
- Remote-control tools such as AnyDesk and TeamViewer. The Service Desk uses Kestrel Assist for remote support. Never allow remote access to your device for someone who contacted you unexpectedly.
- Browser extensions that can read page content, unless they are listed in the approved extensions catalogue in Company Portal.

## 4. Generative AI Tools
You may use Kestrel Copilot, the company's enterprise AI workspace, with any data classified up to Confidential. Consumer AI chatbots and free AI tools may be used only with Public data. Never paste Restricted data, such as customer personal data, credentials, API keys or source code from restricted repositories, into any AI tool. AI-generated code must go through normal code review, and you remain responsible for its licensing and correctness.

## 5. Email and Messaging
Automatic forwarding of work email to a personal mailbox is blocked and must not be worked around. Use Microsoft Teams for internal chat. WhatsApp and other personal messaging apps must not be used for customer data or for decisions that need a record.

## 6. Monitoring and Privacy
Kestrel monitors company devices and networks for security purposes, including endpoint protection alerts, sign-in logs, web proxy logs and data loss prevention events. These logs are kept for 180 days. The content of personal communications is not read routinely; an investigation into an individual needs approval from both HR and the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

## 7. Violations
Breaking this policy may lead to removal of access and disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Report suspected misuse to your manager, or anonymously through the Ethics Line.
